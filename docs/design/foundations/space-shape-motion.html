Space, shape, motion

Nine steps, four radii, three durations, four stacking levels. The bars are drawn at true size, so the distance between a 4 and a 48 here is the distance you will see in the product.

What the steps are for

A spacing scale exists to create grouping, not to fill space. Distance is the only hierarchy cue that costs nothing.

Inside a group: 4px

Two playlists, close enough to read as one list.

Between groups: 32px

The same two playlists, far enough apart to be two things. Nothing changed but the gap.

Shape

Four radii, each drawn at true size.

--radius-artwork4px
Album art. Square, barely softened, so a grid of covers reads as one surface.
--radius-control6px
Buttons and inputs.
--radius-card10px
Cards, the player bar, the empty state.
--radius-chip999px
The only pill in the system, and only for a genuine filter chip.

Motion

Transitions only. No keyframes, no decorative loops.

--motion-fast
120ms
hover, focus, the lamp coming on
--motion-base
180ms
state changes a row can show
--motion-slow
240ms
the player bar and nothing else

A reduced-motion preference drops all three to zero. The player bar additionally drops artwork transitions, seek smoothing and hover scale, while keeping the position indicator and the focus ring, which carry information rather than decoration.

Stacking
