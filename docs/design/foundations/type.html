Foundations

Type

One family, IBM Plex Sans, for everything including numbers. Most of what this app says is a track title at 14px, so the scale is built upward from there rather than down from a display size.

The scale

Six sizes. The lower three sit 2px apart and read as a close pair, which is honest: they are metadata, a track title and a sentence. The upper three step by 4, 4 and 8.

Why one family is enough

The measurement that settled it, kept here so the decision is not re-argued on a hunch.

digit advance
Advance width of every digit 0 to 9, at a 1000px em, measured in the browser. All ten are identical, so the figures are tabular by default.

proportional letters
