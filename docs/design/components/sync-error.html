Colour

Every pairing on this surface. What the band is, what it costs, and why the retry is an accent fill are in DESIGN.md.

The fault sentence 5.20:1
The reassurance, and the control that opens the raw words 5.71:1
Sync now 5.85:1

The band costs 24px and nothing else. On the nine-line diagnostic at 390 the band alone is 144px, the diagnostic alone 279px and both together 303px, so the framing costs exactly its own vertical padding. The cap on the raw block is what makes the fourth case drawable: its content wants 283px, and uncapped the component is 483px, which leaves 1 row of list instead of 4.

What is not drawn

A timestamp. The header shows PlaylistSchema.lastSyncedAt and the sync summary shows SyncRun.finishedAt, and those are different times.

Whether a retry is available, and that is a server question. The third case drops the retry because retrying cannot work on a deleted source, and the rule is the track row's: offer retry only where retrying can work. But nothing in the contract says which failures are worth retrying, so a client cannot derive it from SyncRun.status alone.

The error sentence in the header. The header carries the state and the time and nothing else, so the news is on screen once. The three-way split is in DESIGN.md.

A fault that is not a sync failure. A share or a rename can fail, and nothing in the contract gives those a record like SyncRun gives this one.

Usage

Do say what was not lost whenever there is something to lose, because a sync never deletes and a user who has been told their music can vanish is reading this surface with more fear than the fault deserves. Do keep the reassurance on its own line and in --muted, so the thing that settles the alarm is not printed in the alarm colour. Do omit the reassurance when there are no tracks, and do not offer a retry on a failure that retrying cannot fix. Do keep the mapped sentence and put the server's words behind the control, never in the sentence. Do keep the cap and the pre-wrap on the raw block; without the cap an unbounded string is a 483px component. Do not repeat this sentence in the header, and do not let the state carry the fault by colour alone: the word "Sync failed" is what a reader without the colour reads.
