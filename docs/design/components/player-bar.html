PlayLiberty design / player bar
Components

Player bar

The one component whose height is paid for by the thing it covers. It is anchored to the bottom on both surfaces, so every pixel of it is a pixel of track list the user cannot see, and it is the only place in the product where a control has to be worth a second row.

The bar

Two rows. Row one is the thumbnail, the title and the player actions, with the transport on the bar's centre. Row two is the durations and the seeker, capped at 480px and centred with the two times against its ends.

The 40px artwork carries the equalizer strip, because a playlist that is playing says so on its artwork everywhere it appears. Six bars, because 7 * 6 - 4 = 38 is the artwork's content box exactly. The strip's ink lands on an arbitrary image with no scrim, which measures 1.60:1 on a mid grey cover, and the phase table and that price are in DESIGN.md.

Playback state

data-state on the bar, and it drives two things and not a third.

The strip is on the artwork in both drawings because the artwork is a track, and a track that is loaded carries the strip whether it is playing or not: the strip says this is the current track, and the play glyph says whether it is sounding. A real client adds and removes the strip's animation rather than the strip, and reduced motion does exactly that for free, because each bar carries its resting height as a plain scaleY and the keyframes only override it while the animation runs.

The transport is not keyed to it, and that is where the bar differs from the header. The header's steps are absent unless the playlist is playing, because a paused playlist has no previous or next to go to. The bar's are always there, because the bar is the one place a user goes looking for them and a control that appears and disappears there is worse than one that is always present. Change track, and aria-label changes with it: the button is about the track being played, so it is "Pause" and not "Pause this playlist".

Same 98px either way, so the choice costs nothing in height and only in title room: at 1056, 818.4px flush against 375.2px centred, the cost of the centre being 443.2px; at 390, 111px, which is all the flush arrangement has once the transport has come off the middle. The drawn title is the same 339px in every one of them, and only the 390 drawing truncates it. The shuffle is gone at 390 because the header already carries it and a bar that is 348px wide has room for one secondary control, not two.

What is not drawn

The bar with nothing playing. It is persistent, so it cannot be absent the way the header's transport is, and the answer is a bar with an empty identity and a play that cannot act. That control is disabled rather than merely inert, for the reason check 2p exists.

The width at which the two arrangements swap. The client picks by available width, and the threshold is a judgement rather than a derivable number, so no figure is written into the token set for it.

Usage

Do keep the play filled and larger than the steps. It is the only door, and a row of equal boxes is only defensible while the filled middle is what says which one is the door. Do keep the bar's boundary a hairline and do not reach for the shadow DESIGN.md allows it, because on this theme a shadow lifts nothing. Do size the artwork at 40px, so the bar's strip and the track row's are the same table rather than two of them. Do measure the height before changing anything in it, because a bar anchored to the bottom gives none of it back. Do not put a second line under the title unless it has something to say: TrackSchema has no artist and no album, and the resting file state has no word, so the line every player bar spends on the artist has nothing to put in it here.
