## Design Specificity Verdict

**Authored for this product, not category-interchangeable, in its language; category-interchangeable in its shell.**

The identity is carried by vocabulary no generic player owns: five file-status tones plus a removed-source tone that overrides them, the derived six-hue sleeve, the badge that says "3 pistes ne sont pas enregistrées" beside "A jour", the sync band's `conservée / ajoutée / retirée / échouée`, and reassurance copy whose only job is durability. Strip the labels and the shell is Spotify's grammar: a cover grid with an overlay play, a pill filter row, a bottom transport bar. Specificity lives in the copy and the states, and it is strong there.

**Deterministic scan.** `impeccable detect --json src` returned **0 findings, exit 0** across 69 `.tsx`, 50 `.ts` and 28 `.scss` files. The clean is real (synthetic bad files fire). One caveat: the `repeating-stripes-gradient` rule has no coverage on stylesheets, so the three `repeating-linear-gradient` sites at `PlaylistHeader.module.scss:696`, `TrackRow.module.scss:53` and `PlayerBar.module.scss:66` are invisible to it (it fires only on the built `dist/index.html`).

**Visual overlays.** The detector overlay is live in the Chrome tab currently showing the Étoiles detail page; the live server (port 8400) was stopped and `git status` is clean. In-page findings: 2 on the detail and 1 at 390. Of these, `first-viewport-column-overflow` (one column at 197% of viewport height) is a false positive, the track list is supposed to be tall; the 42px `text-overflow` on a row title is the intended ellipsis. The real one is `name-text` overflowing by 22px at 390, which is the mobile truncation reported below. axe-core 4.13.0 found **0 violations** at 1440, at 390 and on the detail. One 404 on the library: `GET /api/v1/tracks/dff084c1.../thumbnail`.

## Overall Impression

This is a disciplined, unusually thoughtful interface. The theming system does what its document claims, the dialogs are built to a standard most apps never reach, and the copy states the product's one hard promise where it matters. The failures cluster in two places: the product's core verb (start playback, sync, recover a failure) is the least legible thing on screen, and the 390 width is where the interface most often lies about what it contains. The single biggest opportunity is to stop treating diagnostics as content: the sync band, the skip switch and the raw failure string occupy premier space while `Synchroniser` hides in an overflow menu.

## What's Working

1. **The state vocabulary.** Every tone carries a word, a removed source takes the removed tone whatever the file status, and no state relies on colour. Verified on the Failed, Downloading and no-art rows, and on the sleeve hues that read as "no artwork" rather than as a state.
2. **Dialog craft.** `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal`, labelled and described, focus on the task field, Tab trapped with wrap, Escape and scrim click to close, focus returned to the opener, and the checkbox hint as the accessible description.
3. **Measured contrast and focus.** Every control I checked carries the same visible ring, including the invisible row hit layer. Floor measurements hold: muted on surface 5.71:1, accent on surface-raised 4.92:1, failed tone on surface-raised 4.74:1, control border on surface-raised 3.42:1.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] A deferred delete is silently discarded when you leave the screen.**

- **What**: `useDeferredAction` commits in a `setTimeout`, and its cleanup is `useEffect(() => clear, [clear])` (`useDeferredAction.ts:96`). Delete a playlist, open another one inside the 6s window, and the timer is cleared: the toast disappears, `commit` never runs, the playlist is still there, and nothing is said.
- **Why it matters**: a destructive action reported as done that never happens breaks the user's model of their own library, which is the one thing this product promises to keep.
- **Fix**: hold the pending action above the route (a module store or a route-scoped provider) so it commits on unmount, or commit immediately and make the toast a true undo. Add a test that unmounting mid-window still commits.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] On a phone the playlist name truncates and four filter states are clipped.**

- **What**: at 390 the card is 160px and its name row holds the name, the lock and the incomplete badge, so "Étoiles" renders "Étoi...". The detector agrees (22px overflow). In the same view the chip rail shows 2 of 6 states, cuts the second mid-word behind the sort control, and the chips are 36px tall, which breaks `DESIGN.md`'s own 44px target rule.
- **Why it matters**: the playlist name is the identity, and the filter is how a user reaches the three failed tracks, which is the one job the phone screen must do well.
- **Fix**: move the badge out of the name row into the meta line (it already lives there on a tile); hide zero-count chips; give the rail a real overflow affordance; give the chips the 44px box the header's icon buttons already grow.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable adapt`

**[P1] A failed download leads with the vendor's string and cannot be acted on in bulk.**

- **What**: each failed row shows `Échec` over `yt-dlp exited with code 1: ERROR: unable to download video data: HTTP Error 403: Forbidden`, in red, at ~11px, ellipsized, with the full text only in `title`. Recovery lives inside each row's overflow. `SyncError` already implements the opposite pattern: a mapped sentence with the raw text behind a reveal.
- **Why it matters**: sync failure is the product's worst day and it lands on a household user who cannot act on `yt-dlp` or `403`. Three tracks cost three menu visits.
- **Fix**: lead with a mapped sentence ("YouTube a refuse le telechargement"), keep the raw reason behind a `Details` disclosure, allow two lines, and put `Reessayer les 3` on the failed filter and in the summary block.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify`

**[P2] The core verbs are the least visible actions; the transport keys are unguarded.**

- **What**: `Synchroniser` is the fourth item of a five-item row menu while `Ajouter une piste` is a prominent header control, so a stale playlist stays stale unless the user remembers where sync lives. In `PlayerBar`, the window `keydown` handler excludes only `input`, `textarea`, `select` and `[contenteditable]`, so Space on any focused button toggles playback instead of activating that button (Enter still works), and a slow or hung queue read leaves the pressed play merely dimmed, with no message and no timeout.
- **Why it matters**: sync is why the product exists, and a keyboard user's most common key is stolen by a global handler.
- **Fix**: promote sync next to the playlist state; guard the transport handler on `event.defaultPrevented` and on the focused control; give the queue read a timeout that lands on the existing `detail.playbackFailed` + retry path.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout`

**[P2] The gallery spends its accent everywhere and paints its covers as holes.**

- **What**: at 390 the library shows seven filled green play circles plus a filled floating action, against a brief of "calm, content-first, quiet" and a design record that reserves the accent for the play control. Covers and the detail hero carry `loading="lazy"`, so the first paint is black and grey squares; `DESIGN.md` says the subject of its own screen must load eagerly.
- **Why it matters**: the artwork is the content, and it currently arrives as a field of empty boxes behind a wall of identical green.
- **Fix**: make the tile play a quiet control that appears on hover and focus, keeping the filled play for the detail and the player bar; seed the cover placeholder with the derived sleeve hue instead of `surface-raised`; load the detail hero eagerly.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable quieter`

## Persona Red Flags

**The household curator (desktop, daily operator).** Deletes a playlist and opens another one; the playlist survives and the toast is gone. To retry three failed tracks he must open three overflows, because `Reessayer` is per-row and `Recuperer en entier` is unexplained. To start a sync he must remember it is inside `Actions pour <name>`.

**Alex (power user).** Space and arrows move the playhead, not the focused control. Six chip tab stops exist, four of which resolve to an empty list plus `Tout afficher`. A 634-track playlist can only be walked by scrolling, since search is a product non-goal.

**Sam (screen reader, keyboard).** Every gallery card's play button announces `Lire cette playlist` with no playlist name, while that card's shuffle and overflow do include it. Focus order per card is play, then name link, then badge, then shuffle, then menu: the action precedes the item. The incomplete badge is a `role="img"` with `tabIndex=0` that does nothing, one extra stop per card.

**Casey (phone, one-handed).** The name reads "Étoi...". Five of six chips are clipped with no scroll affordance. The floating `+` covers the last card's metadata and, on the detail, a row's duration and overflow. The header, the switch, the sync band, the stamp and the chips take about half the viewport before the first track.

## Minor Observations

- `Derniere synchronisation Il y a 3 jours` runs two sentences together with a capital and no separator.
- The band's `26 ajoutees` is a last-run delta with no frame, and it reads against the playlist's own count of 26.
- The band mixes last-run counts with the live failed count, and three of its four cells are zero on a healthy playlist.
- One state, three words: `Stockee` on a chip, `n'est pas enregistree` in the badge tip, `telechargee` in the empty promise.
- `AddTrackDialog`'s `Ajouter` is enabled with no playlist ticked and returns early with no message.
- `AddPlaylistDialog`'s `Ajouter` is enabled on an empty field, and a scrim click discards a pasted URL without confirmation.
- A refused share grant is invisible: no `onError`, and the toggle derives from server state, so it simply does not move.
- The sort control shows only its value; `Trier par` exists only as an aria-label.
- The player bar is 98px while both pages reserve 97px, so about 1px of the last row hides under it.
- The hatched no-artwork sleeve uses a 12px stripe at 236px and reads as a missing-art placeholder rather than a designed sleeve.
- The unpinned gallery group has no heading after the rule, so a heading list shows only `Epinglees`.
- The language control exists only on the library.

## Questions to Consider

1. The product promise is that nothing is lost, yet the delete commits on a timer that navigation silently cancels. Which wins: the undo window or the navigation?
2. `Synchroniser` is the reason the app exists and it is the fourth item in an overflow menu, while `Ajouter une piste` is a header control. Which is the primary action of a playlist detail?
3. If a failure's visible lead is a tool the household user has never heard of, what is that string for, and who is it for?
4. The accent means "this is the action", and the gallery spends it on seven identical actions at once. If every tile shouts, which tile is the action?
5. On a 634-track playlist with search refused by design, what is the intended way to reach track 400?
